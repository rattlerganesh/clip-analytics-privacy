# Clip Analytics: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Clip Analytics is a small private tool. It is used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel "Worth The Watch Clips" and the Instagram account @worththewatchthis, to read the performance statistics of their own posts.

## What the tool accesses
With the owner's permission, and with read-only access, the tool reads:
- Statistics for the owner's own YouTube videos (views, likes, comments, watch time, subscribers gained, and the countries viewers are in).
- Statistics for the owner's own Instagram Reels (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves).

## What the tool does not do
- It does not post, upload, edit, delete, comment, like, follow or send messages.
- It does not access anyone else's account or private data.
- It does not collect personal information about viewers. The audience data it reads is totals by country only.

## How the data is used and stored
The statistics are saved in files on the owner's own computer and are used only to produce the owner's own performance reports. They are not sold, shared or given to anyone else. Sign-in tokens are stored privately on the same computer.

## Removing access
The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook Settings under Business Integrations (Instagram).
